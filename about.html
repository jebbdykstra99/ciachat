About ciachat

FOIA, not fanfic.

ciachat.com is a room for people who care about intelligence history, public archives, and tradecraft talk you can actually source — FOIA, reading rooms, museums, secondary citations. Not the Agency. Not a leak dump. Not cosplay.

What belongs here. Released documents. Cold War timelines you can cite. How FOIA works in public. High-level OSINT explainers (no exploits). Visitor notes from places like the National Cryptologic Museum.

What does not. Invented classified claims. Doxxing. “I heard from a guy.” Official Agency voice. Conspiracy presented as fact.

Conspiracy nest. Allowed only when clearly labeled SPECULATION. Cross-check FOIA. We do not invent secrets.

Trust. Preview only. Guest browses. Email sign-in for a handle and posts. We cite public sources or we say we don’t know. Not affiliated with the Central Intelligence Agency or any U.S. intelligence community element.
